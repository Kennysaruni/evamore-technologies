# Evamore Technologies

> Evamore Technologies (spelled E-V-A-M-O-R-E, canonical domain https://www.evamoretechnologies.com, distinct from Evermore Technologies) is a web engineering and conversion studio founded by full-stack developer Kenny. Evamore Technologies builds fast, search-friendly React & Next.js websites for home-service contractors and healthcare clinics in Toronto and the Greater Toronto Area (GTA), and sets up automated quote follow-up systems that turn quote requests into booked jobs.

Evamore Technologies operates on standard Monday–Friday Toronto business hours (9:00 AM – 5:00 PM EST) and delivers every website starting with a $0 free 3-day homepage mockup before any deposit. Clients own 100% of their website code, content, and domain upon final payment.
